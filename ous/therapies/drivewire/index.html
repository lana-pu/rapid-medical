Indications for Use. The Drivewire 24 Guidewire is intended for general intravascular use, including the neuro and peripheral vasculature. The Drivewire is intended to facilitate the selective placement of diagnostic or therapeutic catheters. This device is not intended for use in the coronary arteries.

Contraindications. Drivewire™ 24 Guidewire is contraindicated for patients with known hypersensitivity to nickel-titanium and patients with stenosis and/or pre-existing stent proximal to the target site that may preclude safe navigation of the device.
