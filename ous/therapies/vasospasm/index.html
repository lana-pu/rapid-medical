Take command in vasospasm

COMANECI™ is the only active assist device with the versatility to treat wide-neck aneurysms and restore arteries in vasospasm, providing results without permanent implants.

Request a demo

Cerebral vasospasm is an orphan disease, and leading cause of death and disability following subarachnoid haemorrhage (SAH)¹˒², yet lacks a defined treatment strategy. COMANECI is the first device to receive FDA breakthrough designation to provide options for these patients.

Restore the flow with

active control

Unlike other angioplasty devices, COMANECI's active technology offers precise control over vessel dilation with full visibility, potentially reducing the risk of vessel injury.¹˒²

Gradual expansion can achieve similar results as high-impact balloons – with less damage to the vessel.³

Continuous

blood supply

Unlike balloons, COMANECI provides support without blocking blood flow to the brain. Clinicians can focus on accurate placement rather than the countdown to an ischemic event. COMANECI demonstrates the highest complete occlusion and lowest complication rates.

See the comparison
Enable

combination therapy

The patent design of COMANECI allows dual treatment — mechanical dilation plus the concurrent infusion of vasodilators. Synergistic action may enhance efficacy compared to a single treatment.

The proof is in

the patients

In a multicenter study, severe vessel constriction improved significantly, with 52% of patients achieving favorable outcomes at 6 months.²

Product overview

COMANECI™ is designed with active, responsive technology to enable non-occlusive coil embolization procedures.

  • Non-occlusive design

    Unique braiding technology prevents flow disruption during the coiling procedure.

  • Complete visibility

    100% radiopacity enables precise positioning & visualization throughout the procedure.

  • Proprietary active technology

    Operator-controlled adjustability enables adjustable vessel conformity.

  • Low profile delivery

    Easy navigation in difficult-to-reach areas.
