Product overview

The world's first and only adjustable stent retriever, TIGERTRIEVER™ is designed with active, responsive technology for patient-specific procedures.

  • Reinforced distal protection

    Distal Embolic Net (“TIGER DEN”) helps protect against distal emboli and provides the lowest ENT rate of any mechanical thrombectomy trial (2.6%).¹

  • Complete visibility

    Radiopacity improves first-pass success with precise positioning & visual feedback.⁶˒⁷˒⁸

  • Proprietary active technology

    The only clot retriever equipped with real-time adjustability – accelerates clot integration and removes tension on the vessel.

  • Small microcatheter compatibility

    Lower profile microcatheter (MC) compatibility and lower delivery forces.

Indications for Use. The Tigertriever Revascularization Device is intended to restore blood flow by removing thrombus from a large intracranial vessel in patients experiencing ischemic stroke within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for thrombolytic drug therapy or who failed thrombolytic drug therapy are candidates for treatment.

Contraindications. Use of the Tigertriever Revascularization Device is contraindicated under these conditions: Patients with known hypersensitivity to nickel-titanium. Patients with stenosis and/or pre-existing stent proximal to the thrombus site that may preclude safe recovery of the Tigertriever Revascularization Device. Patients with angiographic evidence of carotid dissection. Refer to the Instructions for Use for complete indications, contraindications, warnings and precautions.
