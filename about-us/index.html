About Us

We are a global community headquartered in Northern Israel focused on one unified goal – to pioneer solutions that expand what’s possible in neurovascular therapy.

What we do

Utilizing proprietary manufacturing techniques, we develop the only responsive, remotely adjustable interventional tools to treat ischemic and hemorrhagic stroke.

Leadership Team

Our leadership team combines several decades of global MedTech experience to advance neurovascular technologies, for optimized stroke procedures and better patient outcomes.
