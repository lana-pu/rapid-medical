Visual principles

One expressive organic form at a time, precise composition, generous negative space and a disciplined hierarchy. Color appears primarily as a focused source of light rather than a decorative layer.

Design language

Existing device renders and approved clinical imagery are supported by selected abstract art direction. The website can remain visually cohesive using static imagery and modest motion.
