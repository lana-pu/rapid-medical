Visual principles

Generous breathing room, softly defined surfaces, selective color, structured typography and controlled highlights. The visual hierarchy remains deliberate, even when color becomes expressive.

Design language

Existing medical photography and device imagery are supported by subtle atmospheric treatments. Clean graphic components carry the visual identity across the site.
