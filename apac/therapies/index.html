Ischemic Stroke

87% of all strokes are ischemic* and caused by a blood clot that disrupts blood flow in the brain. Active thrombectomy with TIGERTRIEVER™ enables fast revascularization and real-time adjustability for better patient outcomes.

Hemorrhagic Stroke

Hemorrhagic stroke is caused by a blood vessel that ruptures in the brain. COMANECI™ is the only non-occlusive, active assist device with the versatility to treat wide-neck aneurysms and restore arteries in vasospasm, providing results without permanent implants.
