Tigertriever training: offline package test
============================================

This is a technical test, not training content. It checks whether a training package
can open and play on your hospital computers without the internet.

Please try it on each type of computer the physicians and representatives will use.

1. Copy the ZIP to the computer and extract it (right-click > Extract All on Windows,
   double-click on a Mac). Do not open the files from inside the ZIP.
2. Turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the network cable.
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click index.html.
   Press Play on both videos.

The page shows a list of checks. When it says "The package works on this computer",
it works. In any case, press "Copy results" and paste the text into an email to us.

If something is blocked, please also tell us:
- Was the ZIP allowed onto the computer (email, USB stick, download)?
- Did a security warning appear? What did it say?
- Which browser opened the page?

The two clips are stand-ins taken from the public Rapid Medical website.
