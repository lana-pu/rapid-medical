**Purpose:** Validate discrepancies between the original client content draft, October wireframe, the selected Option #3 proposal, and our v0.6 Feature Inventory. These questions concern factual deliverables, content coverage and distribution, *not* UX layout choices. The main meeting guide v0.6 already covers routine release/workflow questions; this is the essential supplement.

1. **Accepted proposal** (`SCOPE-09`): Can you share the final written Option #3 specification that was approved with the PO? We need to distinguish included features from exploratory options.
2. **Interactive T13 knob** (`SCOPE-01`): Was the manipulable precision-knob / expand-relax illustration agreed as a deliverable, or only suggested as a possibility?

4. **Additional deliverables** (`SCOPE-03`, `SCOPE-04`): Is the single WhatsApp video still required? Should training run at a hosted learner-facing URL as well as inside the downloadable offline version?
5. **Device and market coverage** (`SCOPE-07`): Are Tiger 13/17 the first launch, with 21/25 later? Does 17U belong to first or later coverage, and which markets actually need content?
6. **Media readiness** (`SCOPE-08`): Can you supply a current stage × model × handle asset inventory, including review-needed and not-yet-filmed clips?
7. **Before-you-begin content** (`SCOPE-06`): Is the Europe/Tiger 13 anesthesia item only a wireframe placeholder, or is there approved medical content for it? If unknown, identify the content owner.
8. **Target screens** (`SCOPE-05`): Which real learner devices/OSs require responsive support, and which will actually be used at hospitals?
