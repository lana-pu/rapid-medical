# Rapid Medical — Marketing Meeting: Information We Can Collect Now
2 November 2026 · UX-led factual discovery, not clinician user research

**Constraint:** Marketing is the only direct respondent until a prototype is available. We will not ask them to make UX layout decisions or to approve clinical/regulatory claims. Unknown answers can be captured as `NOT KNOWN — OWNER NEEDED` without blocking concept design.

## Suggested live discussion (prioritise 01–08)

### 01 · First release and success
**Ask:** Which specific configurations and regions will be included in the first release? What is the minimum successful result?
**Evidence to request:** Release plan / initial coverage matrix.
**Related register IDs:** MKT-01; MED-01; MED-02.
**Boundary:** Do not infer clinical market approval.

### 02 · Current content sample
**Ask:** Could you show one current course and its editable source files, including the sequence and media?
**Evidence to request:** Actual source deck/folder (de-identified if needed).
**Related register IDs:** MKT-03; MED-03.
**Boundary:** Source materials still need clinical approval.

### 03 · Asset readiness
**Ask:** For the new T13 handle and existing configurations, which clips are ready, missing or awaiting review?
**Evidence to request:** Asset inventory with statuses.
**Related register IDs:** MED-06; ED-07.
**Boundary:** Do not infer equivalence from shared clip.

### 04 · Which comparison content exists
**Ask:** What differences between models or handles are already documented in approved materials?
**Evidence to request:** Reviewed comparison sources and owner.
**Related register IDs:** MED-05; REP-03.
**Boundary:** Marketing cannot approve clinical claims.

### 05 · Who edits and approves
**Ask:** Who will enter text/video, review it, approve clinical accuracy and download the package?
**Evidence to request:** Names/roles and existing review process.
**Related register IDs:** MKT-02; ED-04; MED-08.
**Boundary:** Avoid asking for UI layout preference.

### 06 · Current update example
**Ask:** Can you show a recent instance where an asset changed and which pages or regions needed updating?
**Evidence to request:** Real example of asset reuse.
**Related register IDs:** ED-01; ED-02; MKT-03.
**Boundary:** Proxy knowledge if marketing not actual editor.

### 07 · Usage known from marketing
**Ask:** From training sessions you have observed, how are materials presented and what questions tend to arise afterward?
**Evidence to request:** Observed examples, not assumed physician interview.
**Related register IDs:** REP-01; REP-02; REP-06.
**Boundary:** Mark as proxy evidence.

### 08 · Distribution & offline
**Ask:** How do teams currently send and launch offline training, and which machines do they use?
**Evidence to request:** Example of existing handoff / package.
**Related register IDs:** OPS-01; OPS-02; OPS-04.
**Boundary:** Actual OS/browser constraints to verify later.

### 09 · Regional publishing
**Ask:** Who confirms which package is valid for which market, and is the intention one regional ZIP or several?
**Evidence to request:** Release scope policy / source owner.
**Related register IDs:** MED-02; OPS-04.
**Boundary:** No automatic OUS=EU assumption.

### 10 · Instructions, warnings and IFU
**Ask:** Who will supply approved IFUs, warnings and relevant version identifiers?
**Evidence to request:** Approved source files and medical contact.
**Related register IDs:** MED-04.
**Boundary:** No marketing medical sign-off.

### 11 · Release corrections
**Ask:** How do you currently handle corrections when earlier training files have already been distributed?
**Evidence to request:** Current distribution/replacement example.
**Related register IDs:** OPS-05; ED-09.
**Boundary:** Do not promise remote revocation.

### 12 · Scope check
**Ask:** Is the short WhatsApp video part of this project? What is explicitly out of scope for first launch?
**Evidence to request:** Updated written brief.
**Related register IDs:** MED-09; MKT-04.
**Boundary:** Do not fold into learner app by assumption.

## Capture method
Record the answer verbatim or a concise factual summary, date, respondent role and supporting file/link. Label claims as `MARKETING-FIRSTHAND`, `MARKETING-PROXY`, `CLIENT-APPROVED CONTENT`, or `UNVERIFIED`. A missing approved source is an open dependency, not an invitation to improvise clinical content.

## Not asked in this meeting
- Do not attempt direct physician JTBD interviews through marketing.
- Do not ask the team to choose the navigation, compare composition, editor layout or visual direction.
- Do not treat product claims or public marketing pages as final Instructions for Use.

## Post-prototype research gate
After low-fi / interactive designs exist, request task-based clinician usability testing for: selecting configuration, locating a stage, comparing same-stage/handles, interpreting applicability and warning labels. Separately test editors on changing a reused video, impact review and release. These later activities are not prerequisites to drafting the feature list.
