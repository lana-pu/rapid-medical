# Rapid Medical — Feature Inventory & Delivery Evidence
Version 0.6 · 2 November 2026 · Working product-planning artifact

## Access constraint (confirmed 2 November 2026)
Until first design/prototype, direct questions are **only** to Rapid Medical marketing. No clinician or representative interviews are available now. Client-facing questions seek factual operational and content information; marketing is **not** a substitute for clinician evidence or medical approval. Usability testing may be requested once designs exist.

## How to read this inventory
- **Core / Enhancement / Candidate** = product-level relevance; **not** a contracted release commitment. Original $950 budget does not cover this list as a production specification.
- **Delivery status** identifies whether the feature is design-ready, dependent on approved source content, requires a technical spike, or should be held.
- **Evidence status** distinguishes direct client requirement, established constraint, design-derived idea, and unvalidated hypothesis. This is not a statistical confidence estimate.
- Feature IDs are stable. Each maps to JTBD identifiers and question IDs in the separate register. No medical instructions are authored here.

Inventory: 50 feature candidates, including 36 core product requirements, 9 enhancements, and 5 candidate/held ideas.
