Rapid Medical — Client Request → Feature Inventory Audit

Date: 2 November 2026
Baseline: Feature Inventory v0.6 (50 features), July content draft, October client wireframe, approved Option #3 context.
Conclusion: major requirements are present, but the original inventory is not fully reconciled; several proposal and content asks are missing, partial, or mislabeled.

What we verified

  • Direct client authored source: July 2026 Content Draft v0.10 (FOR REVIEW, 14 pages); includes audience, 10 narrative sections, legacy/new handle split, media readiness, one WhatsApp short video.
  • Direct client-origin concept: October 14 Tiger Device Stages wireframe; includes US / Europe / Asia Pacific selector, Tiger 13/17 and later 21/25 as rollout groups, conditional handle selector, eight procedural stages, Europe/Tiger13 prerequisite placeholder. Layout is not an approved UX specification.
  • Agreement context: Rapid Medical approved Option #3 Interactive Learning Tool with a planned PO on October 9. The exact signed line-item scope was not available for audit. Earlier proposal included Quick Reference, handle comparison, an interactive knob demonstration and responsive experience, but some elements were conditional.
  • Current inventory: 50 features, including prior UX expansions.

Important corrections

  1. Do not classify every comparison UI element as an explicit client request. Handle comparison appeared in the accepted concept; same-stage and model/spec comparison are subsequent product/design elaborations, not independently proven client asks.
  2. Quick Reference is a functional promise; a separate Quick Reference mode is not. Keep quick access as core and separately test whether distinct mode is needed.
  3. Interactive knob manipulation is not passive video. Add as scope-review feature linked to accepted proposal; do not automatically commit it to production scope.
  4. One WhatsApp short video is an explicit July draft deliverable but may be outside approved Option #3. Flag for scope reconciliation rather than deleting it.
  5. Online learning page plus offline version is implied by July draft; current design has offline learner + online editor only. Treat hosted learner as an open scope question.
  6. No voiceover and non-promotional physician training are explicit draft framing constraints; enforce in content/design acceptance.
  7. Tiger 17U and the 13/17 → 21/25 rollout must be tracked explicitly in coverage/readiness rather than assumed from a generic catalog.
  8. Marketing confirms factual inputs and scope; UX decides interaction patterns. Clinical review is separate.

Scope and provenance rules

  • Client explicit: original July deck, original October model/region/content wireframe, direct operational ask.
  • Accepted Option #3: the selected concept was approved, but individual line items are not independently validated as committed deliverables without final proposal.
  • Design/user-derived: IA, same-stage and model comparisons, review UX, preview, release gates, alternative mobile presentation. They remain legitimate product requirements but are not direct client requests.
  • Source draft: clinical copy, market validity, anesthesia block, regional model eligibility and filmed assets are not published instructions.
  • The larger 50+ feature inventory must not be treated as the full fixed-price implementation contract.
