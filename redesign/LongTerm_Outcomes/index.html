Study · COMANECI™

Long-term outcomes of temporary stent-assisted coil embolization for the treatment of intracranial aneurysms using Comaneci remodeling device

The use of the Comaneci device has a low risk of complications in endovascular treatment of IAs, without procedure-related morbidity and mortality.

  • 97
    Of 100 aneurysm necks bridged successfully

    “In 97 cases, bridging an aneurysmal neck was successful.”

  • 90
    Patients with adequate occlusion at 18 months (Raymond-Roy 1 + 2)

    “At 18 months postprocedure, adequate occlusion (Raymond-Roy 1 + 2) was in 90 patients.”

  • All
    Cases with the device successfully deployed

    “The Comaneci device was successfully deployed in all cases.”

Study at a glance

Patients
100 consecutive patients with incidental compressive and ruptured IAs
Inclusion
Wide-neck aneurysm: neck width ≥4 mm or a dome-to-neck ratio of <2
Devices
Comaneci (n = 35), Comaneci 17 (n = 60), Comaneci Petit (n = 5)
Follow-up
3 and 6 months, 1 year, and 18 months; modified Raymond-Roy classification
Center
Department of Neurosurgery, Brodnowski Masovian Hospital, Warsaw, Poland
Journal
Operative Neurosurgery, 2026

Source: Onikijuk J et al., Operative Neurosurgery 2026

Product
COMANECI™
