Study · COMANECI™

SUCCESS Study – Final Results

Data demonstrates a high rate of successful aneurysm occlusion when using Comaneci as an assist device in wide neck aneurysms.

  • 95%
    Successful occlusion at 6 months (Raymond Roy I and II)

    “Successful Occlusion (Raymond Roy I and II) at end of procedure was 86%, and 95% at 6 month follow up”

  • 82%
    Good clinical outcome (mRS 0–2) at 6 months

    “good clinical outcome (mRS 0-2) was 73% at baseline, 80% at 30 days follow up and 82% at 6 months follow up”

  • 0
    Symptomatic thromboembolic events

    “Intraoperative thromboembolic events were reported in 5 subjects (5.5%), none were symptomatic”

  • 0.3%
    Coil entanglement

    “coil entanglement occurred for 1 coil out of the overall 356 coils (0.3%)”
